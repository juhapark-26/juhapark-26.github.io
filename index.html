01 / ABOUT

Signals tell a story.
I help machines listen.

I am an M.S. student in Electronic and Information Engineering at Jeonbuk National University, advised by Prof. Sang Jun Lee. My research connects computer vision, biosignal processing, and deep learning.

My current focus is robust non-contact stress estimation using remote photoplethysmography (rPPG). Previously, I worked on dental and maxillofacial analysis from panoramic X-ray images.

Long term, I aim to bring physiological sensing into egocentric-view and wearable-camera settings—making health-aware computing work naturally in the real world.
